About Us

Creating meaningful opportunities for young students to discover the power and excitement of mathematics.

Making Mathematics Accessible & Exciting

Axiom Initiative is a nonprofit organization dedicated to supporting mathematics learning for students in grades 3 through 8.

We organize competition math mock tests, educational activities, and learning opportunities that help students strengthen their mathematical thinking, problem-solving abilities, and confidence.

Our goal is to create a positive environment where students can challenge themselves, learn from mistakes, and develop a lifelong appreciation for mathematics.

Bryan Kuang Founder and President

Bryan Kuang is a passionate math enthusiast, competition math tutor, and Founder and President of Axiom Initiative. An AIME qualifier since sixth grade, he has earned recognition in AMC and BMT competitions. Through Axiom Initiative, Bryan combines his love of mathematics with leadership and community service, inspiring younger students to develop their mathematical skills and confidence.

Devin Li Co-President

Devin is a junior at Highland Park High School. He is a USAJMO Honorable Mention and a three-time AIME qualifier. He has also competed in several college-hosted math competitions, placing 4th in Algebra at the Stanford Math Tournament (SMT) Online and the Berkeley Math Tournament (BMT) Online， respectively.
